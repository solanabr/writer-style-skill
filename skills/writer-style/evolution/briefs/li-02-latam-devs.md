## Fact-sheet (FROZEN — imported subsets of testbed/briefs/02 + 03; do not re-derive)

- Brazil: the central bank has stated roughly 90% of Brazilian crypto flows are stablecoins
  (Campos Neto, 2024).
- Remittances: LATAM + Caribbean inflows on the order of $150B/year; traditional corridor cost
  averages ~6%. A stablecoin transfer on Solana settles in seconds for a fraction of a cent.
- Brazil solved domestic instant payments with PIX; the dollar-access and cross-border legs are
  the open problems stablecoins actually address.
- The free course: "Solana Development Fundamentals" — 6 modules over 8 weeks, self-paced;
  prerequisites: comfort in any programming language; outcome: a program deployed to devnet + a
  frontend, a portfolio piece. Weeks 2–3 (Rust) are the hardest stretch.
