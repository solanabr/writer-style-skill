## Fact-sheet (FROZEN — imported from testbed/briefs/02-macro-stablecoins-fx.md; approximations
must be styled as approximations)

- Stablecoins are tokenized dollars; USDT + USDC dominate (combined supply on the order of $250B+
  as of 2025 reporting).
- The emerging-market use case is dollar access without a US bank account: savings hedge + cheap
  cross-border settlement.
- Brazil: the central bank has stated roughly 90% of Brazilian crypto flows are stablecoins
  (Campos Neto, 2024). The real traded roughly 4.8–6.2 BRL/USD across 2020–2025.
- Brazil already solved DOMESTIC instant payments with PIX (free, instant, launched Nov 2020).
  Stablecoins matter for the dollar store-of-value and cross-border legs PIX does not cover.
- Argentina: the peso lost >90% of its dollar value 2019–2024; USDT is widely used for savings.
- Remittances: LATAM + Caribbean inflows on the order of $150B/year; traditional corridor cost
  averages ~6%.
- A stablecoin transfer on Solana settles in seconds and costs a fraction of a cent (base fee
  5,000 lamports per signature).
- FX-hedged dollar yield from a phone is a product local retail banking there does not offer.
