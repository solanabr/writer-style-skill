After deciding what tools I to use, I needed to break down the tasks of building the app into smaller tasks. Tasks that could be done in a few minutes or hours every day, during the time I allocated to work on this app. This is one of the hardest parts and where a lot of people give up.

My approach for this step was to break the big task into 2. Then, focus on one of them and break that into other ones. You can do something like that in a couple of minutes. If it gets hard, you can take a small break and then go back to it.

A tip for this stage is breaking down the tasks before you go to sleep. While you sleep, your brain will be processing that information and you might be surprised with the ideas you have on the next day.
