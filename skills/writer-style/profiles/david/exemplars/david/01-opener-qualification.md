If you have a role in your job that require you to do lots of tasks and have lots of commitments, or if you are somebody that wants to know an email and calendar organization that can save your company lots of money, this article is for you.

One of the roles I have in my job is to help other people get unblocked. That meant that I receive tasks from all over the place and it was hard to keep track of all the commitments I made.

Having an organized email inbox changed everything. I will lay down 3 tools from Gmail that I use to keep my tasks organized and that made my workflow much better. They are Archive, Mark as unread/read, and Snooze.
