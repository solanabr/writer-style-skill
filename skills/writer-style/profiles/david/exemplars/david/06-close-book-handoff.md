Book recommendation

In every article I write, I will recommend a book that “changed my life” for you that read it until the end. The book will be partially related to the subject and I believe that it can be a valuable source of information for the ones who read it.

The book recommendation for this article is:

A Mind for Numbers: How to Excel at Math and Science (Even If You Flunked Algebra)

This book will teach you how to learn new stuff more efficiently and get good at them by understanding how your brain absorbs and processes information, even if these subjects don’t come naturally to you.

Let me know your thoughts about this article in the comments!! If it helped you, it might help someone else you know as well, so don’t be shy and share these techniques with them as well :)
