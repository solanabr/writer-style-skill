The last tool I use to keep my tasks organized is the Snooze function. This tool basically removes an email from your inbox and put it back after some time. I use it especially for replying to emails on tasks ETAS. Let me give you an example:

Let’s say that I have a task to do and on the email, I say the ETA is Friday, June 26. What I do in this case after I send this email and receive the acknowledgment is snoozing it until Friday, June 26, at 8 am, so when I start to work on Friday, the email will be on my inbox and I won’t forget to address it.

You can also go to your “emails sent box” and snooze the email you sent with your task’s ETA. That way even if nobody acknowledges it, you will still not forget to reply to it and keep your commitment.
