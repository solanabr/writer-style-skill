Your inbox is a place where you may receive lots of requests from several different places and that if not well organized, will cause you unnecessary mental load every day. How do I keep my inbox organized?

First, archiving everything that I already addressed, that means that all emails that I already read, and don't have to take actions upon should not be on the inbox.
