## Rule pathologies (architecture lens)

1. **The removal test is an entropy filter** — personality never survives a necessity test; it directly
   contradicts deslop's preserve-list and is the engine of game-changer 5/8→0/8.
2. **Caps read as bans**: every marker has a `fallback:` (what to write instead); nothing says spending is
   good. Budgets need FLOORS + positive framing ("~1/2500w IS him"); shame-notes (STAMP history) out of
   writer-facing files.
3. **Identity gate over-reach**: gate the stakes-framing/flag-waving, EXEMPT first-person lived receipts
   (his own payroll runs, his own community) — biography is evidence, not pandering.
4. **"Edges hot, middle calm" is job-blind** — killed the course-opener, whose mid-piece warmth IS the payload.
   Temperature curve should be per-job (routing already classifies jobs).
5. **Every piece samples every register** = uniformity one level up. Humans have per-piece MOODS. Replace
   several per-section quotas with one declared per-piece mood that scales seam density/heat/marker appetite.
6. **The 13-move "reproduce always" engine is the real stamp** (both rounds' 03 have a section literally
   titled "The honest part") — rotation launders tokens on a fixed skeleton. Demote ~half to a mood-sampled
   menu (trade-off always).
7. **~19 simultaneous constraint channels** per section; 5 rotation ledgers; redundant restatements with
   drifted numbers across 4 files (punch ≤5 vs floor 4; antithesis 1/800 vs 2/800). Single-source in the
   card; adding a rule = retiring one.
8. **Pools lack a CONTEXT axis** (course-first/mid/last vs standalone): the teaser critique generalizes —
   the opener pool has NO continuation opener at all, in a pack whose main use is courses.

## Pipeline weaknesses (architecture-of-generation lens)

1. **Fact-sheet order = piece skeleton** (order-isomorphism proven on 01/08; R0-08 and R1-08 share a skeleton
   across different pack states). Fix direction: "re-outline in voice" as Pass B step 0 (facts frozen, order
   free) + per-job shape exemplars.
2. **No fresh-context reader**: B.5 is the author grading its own homework; a separate reviser (draft +
   exemplars only, ≤5 edits + "where I stopped reading", then re-diff) is the only lever aimed at
   passes-lint-but-loses. Also the home of a bounded EDIT pass (pipeline currently can only regenerate,
   never cut — 05 stacks four closing gestures).
3. **No batch coordinator**: parallel writers can't rotate ("assume the reader read your last piece" is
   unsatisfiable in parallel). A cheap Pass-0 dealing each piece opener family/closer family-or-none/seam
   offset/mood + move bans; also the natural home of the series manifest.
4. **Course mode needs a brief `context:` block**: series id, position N/M, prev/next titles, covered-ledger,
   closer contract (point to actual next lesson or plain-close), burned-anecdote ledger.
5. **Calibration WHY never reaches writers**: Round-2 writers won't know R0-08 won. A ≤10-line owner-approved
   LESSONS note (positive targets, cited to rounds, expiring) loaded in Pass B.
6. **Same-model monoculture across the batch**: recurring rhetorical machines ("Read that twice", paragraph-
   final aphorisms) invisible to lexeme checks and canceled out of the A/B by design. A cross-flavor critic
   naming the top repeated machines per batch (advisory, owner arbitrates).
7. **Warmth is unmeasured**: add warmth-side checks (relational-address present in teaching registers,
   verdict-token floor, exclamation floor per card dial, closer-register match) so restraint AND warmth are
   both mechanically visible.
