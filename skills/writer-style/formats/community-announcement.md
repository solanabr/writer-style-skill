# Container: community announcement (Discord / Telegram)

A post in a community's announcement or dev channel, written by its known operator. It probably
pings hundreds of phones — the respect for that ping is the whole register: everything a member
needs, nothing else, scannable in five seconds.

## Contract
- **100–200 words.** Longer means it should have been a blog post with a two-line announcement
  linking to it.
- **The three member questions, answerable on sight**: what do I get · what do I do · when.
  Logistics (date, time WITH timezone, duration, capacity, prereqs) formatted to be scanned —
  short lines or bolded leads, not buried in prose.
- **Operator-casual**: written like a message from the person who runs the place, not a press
  release. The community knows the poster; talking to them like strangers breaks the room.
- **Premise data is sacred**: dates, times, capacities, prereqs from the brief survive verbatim.
  A wrong time in an announcement is worse than any style failure.
- **Emoji: platform-native, functional.** 1–4 on Discord/TG is normal, often as visual bullets
  (📅 🔗). Decorative emoji on every line is bot energy.
- **One link** (`[LINK]` verbatim), placed where the action is.
- **Honest urgency only**: a real capacity limit may be stated once, plainly. Manufactured
  scarcity ("spots filling FAST 🔥🔥") burns operator credibility.

## Reads as AI here (instant fails)
- "We're excited to announce…" / "We are thrilled to share…" — corporate-launch voice in a
  room of people who know you.
- "Don't miss out!" / "Mark your calendars!" filler CTAs.
- A paragraph of mission-statement before the logistics.
- Uniform emoji-bullet walls (every line decorated).
- Hedging ("we hope to see you there, if you can make it!") — operators state the plan.

## Craft notes
- One personal line is often the difference between a bot post and the operator's post ("I'm
  running this one myself — bring your broken deploys"). One. Not a paragraph.
- Close plain or with the community's own established sign-off convention; never both.
