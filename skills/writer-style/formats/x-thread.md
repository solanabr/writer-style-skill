# Container: X thread

5–10 posts, separated in the draft by `---` on its own line. A thread is not a chopped-up blog
post: each post is consumed alone (quoted, screenshotted, seen mid-thread via the algorithm), so
each must stand on its own AND advance the whole.

## Contract
- **Post 1 is the entire funnel.** It states the payoff with a concrete stake or number — never
  announces that a thread exists ("a thread on priority fees 🧵" is a fail; "you're paying for
  compute units you never use — here's how to stop" is the shape).
- **One idea per post.** If a post needs two sentences of setup, the idea belongs one post later.
- **The screenshot test on every post**: readable and worth something out of context.
- **Length rhythm**: most posts ≤280 chars; an occasional longer post is fine on 2026 X but
  uniform long posts read as an essay wearing a thread. Vary hard.
- **Numbers and code**: exact numbers always; code only as a single short line — anything longer
  is described, or belongs in a linked piece.
- **The close** is a verdict or a next action, in-voice. Never "that's a wrap!", never a
  like/RT/follow beg. One CTA max, and it earns its place by the value already delivered.

## Reads as AI here (instant fails)
- Numbered "1/" scaffolding where the NUMBER does the transition work — sequential-listicle
  threads are the canonical thread-boy tell. (Numbering itself is optional; dependence on it is
  the fail.)
- Cliffhanger chaining: "But here's the kicker:", "And that's when it gets interesting:" — a
  chain of teased reveals with no reveals.
- Every post the same shape (claim + colon + elaboration) — vary openings and lengths.
- A "key takeaways" or recap post at the end — the thread WAS the takeaways.
- Symmetric aphorism posts and "Read that again."

## Craft notes
- Threads tolerate texture: one post can be almost telegraphic, another can breathe.
- Place the single best fact/number in post 1 or 2 — most readers never reach post 5; reward the
  ones who do with the sharpest verdict, not with the leftovers.
