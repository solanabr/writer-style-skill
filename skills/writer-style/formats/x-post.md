# Container: single X post

One post, ≤280 characters unless the brief explicitly asks for a longform post. In-feed reality:
the reader sees the first line mid-scroll between two other posts; the post either stops the
thumb with substance or it never existed.

## Contract
- **One idea.** A post carrying two points carries none. Pick the sharpest angle from the facts
  and spend everything on it.
- **The first 6–8 words are the post.** Front-load the claim, the number, or the tension — never
  warm-up ("So I've been thinking about…").
- **Specific beats clever.** A real number, a named API, an exact cost outperforms wordplay.
  "Every CPI call site breaks" > "big changes ahead".
- **Written like a person mid-conversation**, not a billboard: lowercase looseness is allowed,
  contractions expected, a fragment is fine.
- **CTA ≤1, plain.** "link below" / carry the brief's `[LINK]` verbatim. A post that is ONLY a
  link dies; the post must deliver value even if nobody clicks.
- **The link budget is 23, always.** X wraps every URL to a fixed 23-character t.co link. A post
  carrying `[LINK]` (or any URL) keeps its non-link text ≤256 characters (280 − 23 − 1 space);
  never budget the placeholder's literal length. Multi-link: 23×n.
- **No hashtags** (in dev/crypto X they read automated). **Emoji ≤1**, and only if it does work.

## Reads as AI here (instant fails)
- "🧵" / "a thread on…" / "more below 👇" on a single post — thread bait without a thread.
- Payload-free mirrored aphorisms: "It's not X. It's Y." where Y adds nothing checkable — balanced
  halves with ANY joiner (em-dash, comma, colon). One antithesis max per post, only when the second
  clause carries a real fact (the card's false-antithesis cap governs; a dealt contentful contrast
  is the licensed shape) — and if the fact you're reporting is itself a contrast, don't also shape
  the verdict as one; recast it.
- "Just shipped 🚀" / "huge news" / "big day" template energy; corporate-launch voice.
- Engagement bait: "Agree?", "Read that again.", "Most people don't know this."
- Hedged takes ("arguably", "in many ways") — feed posts commit or stay silent.

## Craft notes
- The screenshot test: would this post make sense (and be worth posting) with zero context? 
- A verdict with a visible cost ("migrating took me a day; every CPI line") is the native unit of
  credibility here.
- Character budget is a rhythm tool: one short sentence against one long one beats three medium.
