# Container: blog post

A standalone piece on the author's own surface (blog, Substack-as-blog, docs-adjacent guide).
The default long-form workflow (`../writing-workflow.md`) already governs structure, marker
ledgers, and Pass discipline — this spec adds only the container edges.

## Contract
- **The title promises the payoff, not the topic.** "Stop overpaying priority fees" beats
  "Understanding priority fees on Solana". Never clickbait a promise the piece doesn't keep.
- **The intro is 2–4 sentences of felt problem.** Never "In this post, we will…", never a roadmap
  paragraph — the reader came from a link that already told them the topic.
- **Headings are navigation, not narration.** A skimming reader should reconstruct the argument
  from headings alone; no "Introduction", no "Conclusion" (the deslop lint flags them anyway),
  no cute-but-opaque headings on technical pieces.
- **Code blocks are runnable and minimal.** Exact commands for interesting steps; routine steps
  run terse in prose. Output shown only when the reader must recognize it.
- **Length honesty**: hit the brief's scope by CUTTING SCOPE, not by compressing correctness.
  A 900-word piece covers less than a 2,500-word piece — it is not the 2,500-word piece squeezed.
- **End on the verdict or the next action.** The last paragraph is load-bearing: what should the
  reader believe or do now? No recap of what they just read.

## Reads as AI here (instant fails)
- Roadmap intros and "Conclusion" recaps (the piece restated at both ends).
- Uniform section geometry: every H2 with the same paragraph count and a closing zinger.
- The "What is X? / Why X matters / How to X / Best practices" template skeleton.
- Bullet lists doing prose's job — lists are for genuinely parallel items only.
- "Let's dive in." / "Let's get started." section transitions.

## Craft notes
- One diagram-in-words is often worth a section: walk the reader through what happens, in order,
  with the real names of things.
- Internal links beat repetition: if another piece covers it, link it and move on.
